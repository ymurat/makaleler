# A roadmap for a Metaverse-based digital governance: A case of the Gambia

**Authors:** Pa Sulay Jobe, Murat Yilmaz, Hüseyin Emre Ilgın

**Published in:** PLOS ONE, 2024, Volume 19, Issue 12, Article e0314388

**DOI:** [10.1371/journal.pone.0314388](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0314388)

**License:** CC BY 4.0

## Abstract

This paper explores the integration of Metaverse technologies into the digital governance framework of The Gambia, in order to identify the sectors where these innovations can be implemented most effectively. Driven by the pursuit of cutting-edge solutions to improve governance, improve public service delivery, and promote citizen engagement, the study uses qualitative analysis methodologies, including interviews with Information Technology (IT) professionals and an examination of government reports.
