## Repository Structure

Each paper has its own subdirectory:
- `gambia-metaverse-governance/` - PLOS ONE 2024 paper on Metaverse-based digital governance
- `emotionally-intelligent-virtual-environments/` - Applied Sciences 2024 paper on biosignal-based emotion classification
- `engagement-brand-recall-advergames/` - Applied Sciences 2024 paper on eye-tracking study of advergames
- `eye-tracking-feature-importance-vr/` - Applied Sciences 2024 paper on explainable AI for VR eye-tracking
- `vr-teach-software-development/` - IET Software 2021 paper on VR for teaching software development
- `gamification-software-business-slr/` - SPICE 2017 paper on gamification in software business
- `vr-public-speaking-anxiety/` - EuroSPI 2017 paper on VR exposure therapy
- `yazilim-surec-degisimi/` - Turkish preprint on software process change
